From $1 million to what's possible

Crossing seven figures proves something

You've created something the market wants.

But the road from $1 million to $10 million is different from the road to $1 million. And the road from $10 million to $50 million is different again.

At each stage, the founder has to make different decisions.

What got you here can become the very thing preventing you from getting there.

For two days, we're going to dig into the decisions that can determine what your brand becomes.

There's a point in every successful business where hustle has to become leadership.

Reserve your spot
01

Brand

How do you become the brand people can't stop talking about?

The strongest consumer brands don't simply sell products.

The customer is your compass.

IdentityCommunityEmotionRitualBelonging

We'll explore what makes a consumer brand stand out in an increasingly noisy marketplace — and how you protect the magic as the business grows.

02

Scale

What actually has to change to get to the next level?

The founder who gets a company to $1 million, or one or two locations open, often has their hands in everything. That works. Until it doesn't.

You can't possibly do it all. You can't be good at everything.

We'll talk about the people, infrastructure, leadership and decisions required to scale without creating a company completely dependent on you.

03

Customer Obsession

How do you turn buyers into believers?

Drybar didn't win because women couldn't find somewhere to get their hair blown out. It won because the experience felt different.

From the language to the environment to the tiniest brand details, everything reinforced what Drybar stood for.

We weren't just selling blowouts. We sold the happiness and confidence that comes with it.

We'll explore how memorable customer experiences create loyalty, referrals, community and brands people genuinely want to belong to.

04

Founder Brand

When should the founder step forward?

Some of today's most powerful consumer brands are inseparable from the founders behind them. But becoming the face of your company creates opportunities — and complications.

The founder who builds the company isn't always the CEO the company needs to scale.

We'll explore how to use your story, personality and platform to grow the company without making the entire business dependent on your presence.

05

Money

Capital can accelerate everything — including your mistakes.

BootstrappingOutside investorsStrategic capitalDebtPrivate equity

Every option changes the game.

We'll have candid conversations about raising money, choosing partners, maintaining control and understanding what you're really agreeing to when someone writes the check.

06

Opportunity

The bigger you become, the more opportunities show up.

RetailLicensingCollaborationsNew product linesInternational expansionPartnershipsMediaAcquisitions

The challenge eventually stops being finding opportunities. It becomes knowing which ones deserve a yes.

We'll explore how to recognize the opportunities that can expand your brand — and the shiny objects that can quietly derail it.

07

Exit

Build a company somebody would want to buy.

You don't have to want an exit today to build with one in mind. Understanding what makes a consumer company attractive to potential acquirers can make you a smarter founder right now.

Your greatest strength can become your biggest bottleneck if you don't learn to let go.

We'll talk about enterprise value, founder dependency, brand equity, leadership and what changes when your company becomes bigger than you.

You don't have to know everything. You have to know who to bring into the room.

The room is part of the experience

Imagine being able to say:

We're at $4 million and retail is interested. Are we ready?

And someone across the table has already made that decision.

We have an investor offering us capital, but something doesn't feel right.

And another founder has been through three rounds.

We're growing like crazy, and I'm becoming the bottleneck.

And the person beside you just solved the same problem.

That's a very different conversation than getting generic business advice.

Context changes everything.

That's why we're curating the room.

The bigger the business gets, the less it can depend on you.

Who belongs in the room

This room is for you if…

You're the founder or co-founder of a consumer-facing brand generating $1M+ in annual revenue. You've proven the concept. You have customers. You have momentum. And you're ready to think bigger about what you're building.

You may be:

  • Scaling from $1M toward $10M+
  • Preparing for your next major growth phase
  • Expanding into retail or new distribution channels
  • Considering outside capital
  • Building your executive team
  • Expanding your product line
  • Growing your founder platform
  • Navigating a partnership
  • Thinking about an eventual exit
  • Trying to scale without destroying the culture and creativity that made the company special

Most importantly: you're willing to talk openly about what's really happening inside the business.

This probably isn't your room if…

  • You're still looking for your business idea.
  • You're pre-revenue.
  • You're looking for a step-by-step course on starting a company.
  • Or you just want to collect business cards.

There are plenty of great places for that. This isn't one of them.

The Founders Circle is for people who have already built something real — and want to be around others who have too.
